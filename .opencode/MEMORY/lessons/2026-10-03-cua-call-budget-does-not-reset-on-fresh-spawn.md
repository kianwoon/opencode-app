# Cua call budget does not reset per handoff (2026-10-03)
Three consecutive computer-aid handoffs (one re-run, two fresh spawns) all opened at a non-zero cumulative counter: 29/30, then 30/30, then 31/30 on call 1. Fresh spawns do NOT restore the cua call budget - it tracks a longer-lived scope (session lineage / daemon transport), not the subagent session. Refines the earlier cumulative-per-subagent-session lesson.
Consequence: long GUI batteries starve later hands until the window rolls over or the daemon restarts; a hand opening at ceiling can do almost nothing and will honestly report blocked.
Gate: any GUI battery opens with the first call cua-budget counter reported; pixel-heavy work is planned inside a budget that actually exists; a hand opening at ceiling gets a cua daemon restart (config change + daemon reload + app restart as one unit) instead of a re-fire.
Second finding from the same probes: three baseline cursor readings (3151,1768), (2368,239), (2193,689) with no pixel action issued by the hands - multi-display coordinate spaces and/or user movement; get_cursor_position readings are not comparable across displays; anchor desktop-scope coordinates when work crosses displays.
